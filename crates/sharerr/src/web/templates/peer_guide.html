In Prowlarr

  1. Open Indexers, press +, and pick Generic Torznab.
  2. Name it sharerr (or the name of whoever is sharing with you).
  3. Set URL to the feed URL above, exactly as written. Leave API Path as /api.
  4. Paste your key into API Key.
  5. Press Test, then Save. Prowlarr syncs the indexer to your Sonarr, Radarr, Lidarr or Readarr on its own.

Good to know

  • The key is yours alone. It also lets your torrent client reach their tracker, so there is nothing else to configure.
  • The feed lists only what they are seeding right now. An empty feed fails Prowlarr's test, so if it fails right after setup, wait for them to share something and test again.
  • Their sharerr can see that your key was used and when your client first connected, but never what you search for.
  • If you run sharerr too, ask for their invite instead: pasted on your Friends page, it does all of this and pairs the two instances.
