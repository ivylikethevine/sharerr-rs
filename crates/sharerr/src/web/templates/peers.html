Where friends are

sharerr remembers the last few addresses each friend was seen at, and — when a friend also runs sharerr — exchanges signed endpoint records with them, so one friend noticing a moved address is enough for everyone who already knows them. Records are signed by the friend they describe; nobody can rewrite anyone else's address.
